Title: Firewall Change Procedure
Version: 1.0

Purpose
Describe the approval process for a production firewall change.

Required information
Every change request must include:
- Business justification.
- Source and destination addresses.
- Required ports and protocols.
- Implementation steps.
- Risk assessment.
- Rollback steps.
- Validation steps.

Approvals
A production firewall change requires:
- Peer review by another network engineer.
- Approval and review by security team.
- Approval from the service owner.
- Change manager approval.

Implementation
Changes must take place during the approved maintenance window.

Validation
The implementing engineer must confirm the required connection
works and that unintended access has not been introduced.

Escalation
If validation fails, follow the rollback plan and troubleshooting steps and notify the
change manager.
